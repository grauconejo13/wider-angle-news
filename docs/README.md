# Wider Angle documentation

The Wider Angle is a news-analysis interface that helps readers compare framing,
understand geographic relevance, and distinguish information they can act on from
information they can only monitor or understand.

## Documents

- [Product brief](./product-brief.md) — purpose, audience, promise, and MVP scope.
- [Analysis method](./analysis-method.md) — how story clusters should separate
  evidence, claims, uncertainty, and framing.
- [Technical architecture](./architecture.md) — current frontend and planned
  ingestion and summarization pipeline.
- [Roadmap](./roadmap.md) — staged path from demonstration data to a paid product.

## Product principle

> The news is available everywhere. The product is structured understanding.

The Wider Angle does not promise a perfectly unbiased account. It shows readers
where information came from, what different coverage emphasizes, what remains
uncertain, and how a development relates to their lives.
