# Product brief

## The problem

People often encounter the same events through one dominant source or through
several sources selected to reinforce an existing conclusion. Conventional news
feeds also mix personally actionable information with distant events over which a
reader has no control, producing both tunnel vision and helplessness.

## The product

The Wider Angle groups coverage of one event into a story cluster and presents:

- the details that reporting broadly corroborates;
- claims that should remain attributed to their speaker;
- information that is disputed, incomplete, or developing;
- the aspects different sources emphasize or omit;
- local, economic, political, environmental, historical, and international angles;
- the reader's possible relationship to the event.

It does not replace several news organizations with one supposedly neutral voice.
It provides a transparent surface on which readers can make up their own minds.

## Primary interaction

### My Orbit

Shows stories connected to the reader's selected location, subjects, and practical
ability to respond.

### Wider World

Shows consequential developments outside the reader's ordinary preferences and
geography. This mode prevents personalization from becoming another filter bubble.

Each mode corrects the weakness of the other. My Orbit includes a small
`Beyond Your Orbit` selection, while global stories can explain where they may
eventually touch the reader's life.

## Agency labels

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Act | The reader can take a direct, concrete step. |
| Influence | The reader can participate, vote, contact, support, or spend differently. |
| Prepare | The reader cannot stop the event but can plan around its consequences. |
| Understand | The event is important context even without an immediate response. |
| Observe | No action is presently needed. |

## MVP audience

Curious readers who want to remain informed without surrendering their conclusions
to one channel or carrying the emotional weight of every event.

## MVP scope

- San Antonio, Texas, United States, and world geographic levels.
- Economy, technology, environment, and civic-life subjects.
- Story clusters with expandable structured analysis.
- My Orbit and Wider World modes.
- Prepared demonstration data followed by scheduled live ingestion.

Accounts, comments, chat, continuous breaking-news updates, and automatic
left-versus-right scoring are intentionally outside the first release.

## Commercial premise

Headlines and links remain free. Readers pay for saved preferences, deeper
comparisons, personalized briefings, archives, and carefully produced story
dossiers. Analysis should be generated once per story cluster and cached for all
readers so operating cost grows slowly.
