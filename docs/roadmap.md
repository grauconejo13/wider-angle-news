# Roadmap

## 0.1 — Interaction proof

- My Orbit and Wider World modes.
- Geographic and subject filters.
- Expandable prepared story analyses.
- Responsive interface.

## 0.2 — Live-source proof

- Add GDELT discovery.
- Add a small set of San Antonio, Texas, national, and international RSS feeds.
- Normalize URLs and remove exact duplicates.
- Display real publisher links and timestamps.

## 0.3 — Structured analysis

- Cluster coverage of the same event.
- Produce one cached AI-assisted analysis per cluster.
- Retain source references for every material section.
- Add confidence and review states.

## 0.4 — Personal relevance

- Save a location and selected subjects locally.
- Rank stories using transparent, adjustable rules.
- Preserve Beyond Your Orbit recommendations.
- Explain why each story was selected.

## 0.5 — Revenue test

- Keep headlines, links, and a daily analysis free.
- Test individual paid dossiers and a low-cost annual membership.
- Measure whether readers return for context rather than breaking-news volume.
- Avoid political advertising that could undermine editorial trust.

## Later

- Accounts and cross-device preferences.
- Email briefings.
- Classroom and media-literacy edition.
- Organization and local-business monitoring.
- Human review tools and correction history.

Chat, comments, infinite scrolling, and minute-by-minute breaking-news competition
remain outside the roadmap until user research demonstrates a real need.
